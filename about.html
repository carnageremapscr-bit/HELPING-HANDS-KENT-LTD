Our Story

Professional, Caring, and Trusted Across Kent

Helping Hands Kent Ltd was founded with one simple goal — to provide genuinely reliable cleaning, home care, and property support to the people of Kent. We work with families, landlords, businesses, and vulnerable clients who deserve a team they can truly count on.

Based in Kent and covering the whole county, from Canterbury and Whitstable to Maidstone, Ashford, and the coastal towns, we have built our reputation on showing up, doing the job properly, and treating every client's home or business with the respect it deserves.

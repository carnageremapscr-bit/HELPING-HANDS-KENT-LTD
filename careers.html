What We Look For

Every member of our team is expected to meet the following requirements before starting with us.

  • Full UK driving licence — essential for travelling between client locations across Kent
  • Full DBS (Disclosure and Barring Service) check — required for all roles. If you do not currently have one, it can be obtained before your start date and we can guide you through the process
  • A caring and compassionate nature — our clients trust us in their homes and lives
  • Genuine enthusiasm for helping others and making a positive difference every day
  • Reliability and punctuality — our clients depend on us showing up as promised
  • Good communication skills and a respectful, professional attitude
